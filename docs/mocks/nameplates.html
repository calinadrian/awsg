Nameplate: compact, with auras

Drawn at 3×. The level, name and health sit in one 11px bar; one row of icons sits on its top edge: your debuffs from the left, the mob's important buffs from the right. Every plate is the same 100px. A name that does not fit shrinks a little (6px down to 5px), and only past that shortens with "…".

Health text (Interface settings):

☠3 poison ×3   ❄ chilled   🩸 bleeding: your debuffs, crimson edge    ⚡ enraged: the mob's buff, grey edge
