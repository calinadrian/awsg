Moving the HUD

Right-click any panel for its menu: unlock it, then drag it; it snaps to an 8px grid and the centre lines. Whatever you click comes to the front and stays there. Lock it again from the same menu. "Unlock all" frees every panel at once, and "Lock all" locks them again. Positions are remembered.
